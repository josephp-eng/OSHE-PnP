# Per-machine vision setup checklist

Working notes for the build documentation: every step a builder has to run on **their own**
machine, in order, and when to redo it. Commands run on the Pi from `Software/pnpvision`
with `~/venv-pnp/bin/python -m pnpvision ...`. Status reflects the OSHE-PnP prototype.

| # | Step | Command / tool | Why it is per machine | Redo when | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Pi software | `python3 -m venv ~/venv-pnp`, `pip install --only-binary=:all: -r requirements.txt` | - | new Pi / OS | done |
| 2 | Lens calibration, each camera | `calib_capture.py` (ChArUco 7x5, 8 mm squares, 6 mm markers, DICT_4X4), copy the `.npz` into `calibration/`, then `check-lens` | every lens and sensor differs | lens refocused or swapped, camera resolution changed in crowsnest | done (down, up) |
| 3 | Undistorted Mainsail views + reticle | `undistort-down` / `undistort-up` services (`undistort_stream.py`, add `--motion` for mm ticks) | uses that machine's `.npz` and motion map | after steps 2 or 4 (restart the service) | done |
| 4 | Down-camera motion map | `calibrate-motion --cam down --step 3` on a dark ~3 mm dot, flat and **fixed**, at **PCB-top height** | camera scale, rotation and mirroring depend on the mount (ours: 0.5 deg rotated, ~59 px/mm) | camera moved or refocused | done |
| 5 | Repeatability check (diagnostic) | `repeatability --n 20` on a fixed dot; `--home-every 5` to test homing | belts, motors, frame | mechanical changes, accuracy problems | done (~0.01 mm; sensorless homing 0.1-2 mm) |
| 6 | Visual homing, teach once | `home --teach --polarity bright --thresh 230` with datum dot 0 under the crosshair; then use `home` instead of G28 | sensorless X/Y homing lands up to ~2 mm off; the taught XY **defines the machine frame** | datum or down camera moved (then redo every step below) | done |
| 6b | VISION_HOME button in Mainsail | install the `gcode_shell_command` Klipper extension (KIAUH), then the `[gcode_shell_command vision_home]` + `[gcode_macro VISION_HOME]` sections in printer.cfg (runs `scripts/vision_home.sh` in the background) | paths, user name | new Pi / repo moved | done (KIAUH v6: main menu E) Extensions > G-Code Shell Command; decline the example config) |
| 7 | Down-camera to nozzle offset | `calibrate-offset` (nozzle over a dot by eye, 3 runs); later refine by placing parts and measuring | camera and nozzle-holder mounting | camera or nozzle holder moved | done by eye on datum dot 0 (4 runs, spread +-0.04 mm): (8.056, 53.112) vs CAD (7.71, 53.04). 2026-11-11 after the head was swapped and back: nozzle moved (+0.272, -0.053) mm, offset now (8.328, 53.059) via `--center-only --update-offset` (step 8) |
| 8 | Up-camera motion map + centre | `UP_LIGHT_ON`; nozzle over the up camera at safe Z, then lower it until the tip is sharp (ours: Z38, X84.4 Y137.7); tune the detector with `find-dot --cam up` (no moves) until the red circle is the nozzle tip; then `calibrate-motion --cam up --find-center --step 1` with the same detector options (jogs stay at the focus height, max 3 mm, no safety lift) | up-camera mount and focus | up camera moved or refocused | done 2026-11-10 with `--step 1 --polarity bright --rmin 90 --rmax 130` (tip face): 0.0263 mm/px, camera turned 90 deg, anisotropy 1.035, rms 0.88 px; centre X85.318 Y137.893 at Z38. Steps over ~1 mm fail: off-axis, the tip's side wall hides the face edge and the face merges with the collar. To replace with a target at the part plane + rotation-based nozzle axis (step 6). **After the nozzle or head is remounted:** VISION_HOME, `datum` (up camera drift should be ~0), nozzle to the stored centre at Z38, then `calibrate-motion --cam up --center-only` with a fixed threshold (ours after the remount: `--polarity bright --thresh 150 --rmin 105 --rmax 130`; automatic thresholding broke when the dark nozzle holder filled more of the frame). It keeps the motion map, measures the shift, and with `--update-offset` saves the new centre and shifts the down-camera-to-nozzle offset by the same amount (the up camera and the down-camera frame are both fixed to the plate) |
| 9 | Datum teach / reference | `datum --teach`, `datum --reference` | datum placement on the plate | datum moved | done 2026-11-10: dots fit rms 0.039 mm, rotation 0.034 deg, scale 0.996; up camera at datum XY (61.567, -19.785) |
| 10 | Up-camera light | 6 x WS2812 ring on a free endstop port (ours: EXP1 header, data EXP1_8 = PE15, GND EXP1_9, 5V EXP1_10; STOP/endstop ports did not pass LED data; 3.3 V data, add a 74AHCT125 level shifter if unreliable; later maybe a Pico as a second Klipper MCU), `[neopixel up_light]` + `UP_LIGHT_ON/OFF` in printer.cfg, `[lighting.up] type = "klipper_led"` in `config.toml` | free pins differ per build; endstop ports are filtered inputs and may not pass LED data | - | done, ring lights from EXP1 (2026-11-09) |
| 7b | Offset refined by placing parts | Spare parts on bare board within ~10 mm of each other, off the tape; centre the down camera on one of them (by eye), then `place-test --target X Y --step DX DY --n 5` (down-camera coordinates). It measures that first part's centre as its centre of 180-degree symmetry (thresholding outlined only half a part), keeps its picture masked to a part-sized outline as a template, then after each placement shortlists look-alikes in the wide view around the LAST part picked and checks them close up with the camera over each (lighting varies across the view), taking the best close-up score (real parts 0.78-0.95, pads up to 0.73; threshold 0.70, stop early at 0.85; the run stops after 3 failed picks in a row). Each grab offset seen by the up camera shifts the next picks so grabs converge on the part centre (off-centre grabs tilt the part and it drops). Any part type; a pad that slips through just fails the pick and is skipped. Where parts land is measured by before/after photos of each target (aligned, colour channels evened out, Lab colour difference, size check +-20 %), so tape, pads and the down light's colour flicker don't matter; if that check is unsure, the part is found by its picture plus symmetry centre instead, reported as "ok (backup)" (~0.1 mm less precise) and left out of the offset average. Targets on double-sided tape. Review the summary, then re-run with `--apply-offset` (needs 3+ good placements); check with `--correct` (landing error should be ~0). Images per run in `place_test_<time>/` on the Pi | the by-eye offset and the up-camera centre (found on the holder face) each carry ~0.1-0.3 mm error; this measures their combined effect on real placements | nozzle, head or either camera moved | 2026-11-11: 4 uncorrected runs (8 good placements) gave job error (-0.06, +0.13) mm, scatter a few hundredths; set with `set-offset 8.385 52.930` (was 8.328, 53.059); confirmed with `--correct`: all 5 parts landed within 0.03-0.14 mm, mean about (-0.03, -0.05) mm. Offset kept. DONE |

| 8b | Up-camera centre on the REAL tip | empty nozzle over the up camera at the inspection height (Z34.5), `find-dot --cam up --polarity dark --thresh 40 --rmin 8 --rmax 30` (one blob, the bore, r ~15 px), then `calibrate-motion --cam up --center-only --keep-offset` with the same detector options | step 8 centred on the nozzle HOLDER face; the tip that holds parts is ~0.27 mm off that centre, and rotating parts needs the tip's own axis | nozzle tip changed or bent | done 2026-11-11: centre X85.841 Y137.697 at Z34.5 |
| 12 | Up-camera inspection height | `focus-sweep` with a part on the nozzle, judged on the saved images (the sharpness score is fooled by the nozzle rings) | the real nozzle tip sticks out ~3 mm below the holder face that was used for calibration, so the part's bottom must be put back in that plane | nozzle tip or holder changed | Z34.5 for an 0805 (2026-11-11) |
| 13 | Nozzle rotation | (a) direction: part on the nozzle over the up camera at the inspection Z, `MANUAL_STEPPER STEPPER=stepper_r MOVE=0`, `inspect-up --size L W`, then MOVE=30 and MOVE=60 with `inspect-up` after each (use 30 deg steps, not 90: a chip's angle is mod 180, so +90 and -90 look the same); put `direction` = part angle change per motor degree in `config.toml` `[rotation]`. (b) runout: empty nozzle at the inspection Z, `find-dot --cam up --polarity dark --thresh 40 --rmin 8 --rmax 30` at MOVE=0/90/180/270/360; the bore should trace a small circle. (c) prove it: `place-test ... --correct --rotate-to 0` turns each part the shortest way (mod 180) to the target angle, re-inspects (the offset swings with the part), turns again if more than `--angle-tol` (1 deg) off, and measures the landed angle with the down camera | motor direction vs. camera angles; nozzle tip straightness | rotation motor, belt or nozzle tip changed | 2026-11-11: motor +30 -> part -29.2, -29.5 deg, so `direction = -1`; runout radius ~0.02 mm around (-0.025, +0.044) mm from the crosshair, 0 vs 360 deg repeat 0.007 mm: negligible, no runout table. (c) `--rotate-to 0`: placed -0.6 deg; `--rotate-to 45` (4 parts): landed 4.0-5.0 deg low on every good one, position within 0.12 mm. The motor turns accurately (29.55 deg seen for 29.3 commanded); the up and down cameras disagree by a few degrees, growing with angle (up-camera tilt?). Acceptable for 0805 (reflow straightens chips); fit a correction from a 0/45/90/135 run if needed later. DONE |
| 14 | Board alignment | make the job with the slicer (`Software/Slicer`, Export job, or `pnpslice slice BOARD --side top -o jobs`); put the board on the plate, centre the down camera by eye on alignment pad A (either pad of its part), then `align-board "jobs/<board>-top.job.json" --rot <board rotation, deg CCW>`. ## Machine-specific settings to record
- `config.toml`: travel limits, `safe_travel_z` (larger Z = nozzle lower on this machine), crowsnest snapshot URLs, camera width/height (must match crowsnest **and** the `.npz`), light macros.
- Homing dot detector settings: a white dot on a sparkly or textured print needs a fixed threshold (`--polarity bright --thresh 230`); automatic thresholding failed there.
- Datum detector cut-offs in `config.toml` `[datum]`: `grid_threshold` (grid lines) and `dot_threshold` (white dots) differ; check both with a snapshot after reprinting or relighting the datum.
- Moving or reseating the datum moves the homing dot: re-teach visual homing (`home --teach`), then `datum --teach`; the camera-to-nozzle offset and motion map are unaffected.
- Sensorless homing (printer.cfg): with `driver_SGT 2`, homing at 20 mm/s or 0.8 A false-triggered (stopped early); 40 mm/s at 1.0 A works. Tune SGT before making homing gentler.

## Lessons worth keeping in the docs
- Calibration targets must not move: a loose paper dot slid ~0.05 mm during fast moves and showed up as a fake machine error. Tape or glue it, or use a bolted feature.
- The motion map absorbs camera rotation, mirroring and any X/Y scale difference, so cameras need no mechanical squaring.
- Undistorted images use square pixels (`square_new_K`); OpenCV's default made round dots look 24% oval.
- Addressable-LED data lines run in an unshielded harness next to steppers can corrupt colours (our white down light flickers purple). Fixes: GND wire twisted with data, 220-470 ohm series resistor, 100-1000 uF across LED power, 3.3->5 V level shifter. Matters for vision: purple light makes white features much darker in greyscale.
- The down camera is only ~11.5 mm from the work (undistorted f 681 px / 59 px per mm), so image scale changes ~9% per mm of height: the datum grid (about 0.5 mm off the calibration height) read 4% big, and a 1 deg tilt visibly makes it trapezoidal. Positions must come from centring features, not from reading them off-centre in the image.
- Measure features after centring them on the crosshair (the optical axis): there, a height difference does not shift the position.
- The up camera may not look exactly straight up: on ours, lowering the nozzle from Z20 to Z38 moved it ~1.6 mm off the crosshair. So the up camera is calibrated, and parts are inspected, at one fixed nozzle height (the focus Z), and the nozzle tip can't be jogged over the up camera at normal travel height: vision uses small "stay low" jogs there instead.
